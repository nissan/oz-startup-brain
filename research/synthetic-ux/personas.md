## P1 — Mei: Student founder building an AI prototype

**Design relationship:** Existing core P1; SB1

**Situation:** Has a working university project and wants one useful action before applying for support. Has not clarified ownership with the institution.

**Knowledge and uncertainty:** Comfortable with ordinary web forms. New to grant terminology and commercialisation. Does not know who owns the prototype IP.

**Job to be done:** Leave with a specific question and a person or source to consult, without having to invent personal facts.

**Interaction preferences:** Discreet text input. Short explanations with optional detail. Wants to approve context before it affects the route.

**Trust expectations:** Treats confident eligibility language as a reason to inspect evidence. Needs to distinguish a suggestion from a requirement.

**Exploration behaviour:** Reads the first action, then looks for why it fits. Tries a plain-language label before an unfamiliar technical term. Will set a field to Unknown instead of guessing.

**Likely friction:** Repeating the same clarification or being told to apply before knowing the prerequisite.

**Device and input:** Desktop, 1366×768 CSS pixels, text and pointer; keyboard smoke variant.

**Autonomy boundary:** May edit and inspect. Will not submit an application or contact anyone.

**Privacy boundary:** No nationality, visa status, institution name or personal identity documents are supplied or inferred.

**Desired outcome:** Can explain one next conversation, why it matters, and what remains unknown.

**Later recruitment proxy:** A consenting first-time student founder with unresolved commercialisation questions; no demographic assumptions.

**Starting facts:** stage: working prototype; jurisdiction: deliberately unknown; IP_ownership: unknown.

**Task cards:** T01, T02.
