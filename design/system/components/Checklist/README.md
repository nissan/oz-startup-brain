The exportable route: actions and open questions with their evidence, the confirmed context, corpus version and as-of date, and the standing "information and routing" statement. Ticking an item records personal progress only. Export is a rendering of the response envelope; nothing here needs a server.

Consumer provides: `title`, `items` (`[{text, done, evidence}]`), `context`, `corpusVersion`, `asOf`, `onToggle`, `onExport`, `onEditContext`.
