Oz Startup Brain / Brand sheet

Mark, wordmark and usage

One mark, three lockups, two themes. The mark is the journey: a start node, one bend, a destination filled sage. Every colour is a token; nothing is photographic or generated.

Design baseline • Owner approved 6 November 2026 (#8) • Files in the design system's Logos and Icons groups

Mark

Ironbark tile (`radius-lg` at 64px), paper route, sage destination, on a fixed light specimen ground; the dark variant swaps to the dark ironbark tint on a fixed dark ground. Minimum 16px; the 16px and 32px sizes are the favicon.

Lockups, light variants

Shown on a fixed light specimen ground (paper #f5f0e4), the only kind of ground these files are permitted on.

Wordmark set in Fraunces 600, outlined to paths; tagline in Atkinson Hyperlegible 400 in ink-muted. Horizontal for headers and the OG image; stacked for square or narrow placements; tagline lockup only on the landing page and social previews.

Lockups, dark variants

Shown on a fixed dark specimen ground (paper #1c1a16), the only kind of ground these files are permitted on.

Every lockup exists in a light and a dark file. Light files go on paper or paper-raised only; dark files on dark paper or dark paper-raised only. Neither goes on ochre, plum, sage, a photograph or a mid-tone. Clear space around any lockup is four route-stroke widths (22px at the 64px mark).

Icons and rules

180px touch icon · 32px favicon · 512px manifest icon
  • Never recolour the mark outside its two themes.
  • Never place it on ochre, plum or sage fills.
  • No rotation, shadow, gradient or outline.
  • No "OSB" in running copy; the name is always written out.
  • No photography, illustration or generated imagery in the brand.
